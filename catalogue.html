About Us

Your Trusted Partner
in Marine Technical Service

Marine Core Co., Ltd. is a certified marine technical service provider headquartered in Busan, the heart of Korea's maritime industry. Our team of experienced engineers and certified technicians delivers precise, reliable solutions across all aspects of vessel maintenance and compliance.

From routine inspections and emergency repairs to precision calibration and name-change services, we support ship operators with efficiency and regulatory expertise — minimizing vessel downtime while ensuring full compliance across international flag states.
